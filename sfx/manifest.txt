# Assembly Vale — SFX manifest (canonical). One clip per line:
# file | event id | description of the sound | usage context
# Event ids are the keys of the SFX map in js/audio.js; js/app.js calls AudioMod.play(id).
# Clips are 48 kHz mono Opus from MOSS-SoundEffect v2.0; when a clip is missing or still
# loading, audio.js synthesizes the same event with WebAudio, so the game never goes silent.
# and move into manifest.json (which audio.js loads) once the clip has been generated.
ui-click.opus | ui | Short soft wooden button click, gentle fingertip tap on a small hardwood toggle | Reserved generic UI acknowledgment (menu buttons are silent today; kept for future UI use)
tool-clink.opus | tool | Single bright metallic clink of a steel wrench tapped against a workbench vice | Selecting a tool (Belt, a machine recipe, Rotate, Remove, Upgrade) by button or B/M/R/X/U key
place-thunk.opus | place | Solid wooden crate set down on a concrete workshop floor, low resonant thunk | A belt or machine is placed on the board (rules event "place")
remove-scrape.opus | remove | Wooden plank pulled free with a quick dry scrape ending in a soft thud | A building is removed and half its cost refunded (rules event "remove")
rotate-ratchet.opus | rotate | Two quick crisp mechanical ratchet clicks, small metal pawl turning | A belt or machine is rotated one quarter turn clockwise (rules event "rotate")
upgrade-servo.opus | upgrade | Rising electric servo whir building up and ending with a metallic latch snap | A machine is upgraded to level 2 or 3 (rules event "upgrade")
invalid-buzz.opus | invalid | Short dull rubbery error buzz, low muted electric honk through a small speaker | Any refused command (rock, occupied cell, no gold, build limit, locked recipe, max level); status line explains why
craft-hammer.opus | craft | Small hammer tap striking a metal machine part, bright ringing clink with short decay | A machine finishes a recipe and moves the product to its output slot (rules event "craft")
deliver-chime.opus | deliver | Vintage cash register chime with a brief jingle of coins dropping into a metal drawer | A good arrives at the Exchange and is sold for gold (rules event "deliver")
contract-stamp.opus | contract | Heavy rubber stamp thunk on paper followed by a warm small bell chime | A contract line reaches its quota and the 150-point bonus is awarded (rules event "contract-complete")
spoil-splat.opus | spoil | Wet squelching splat of overripe produce hitting the ground, soft decaying thud | A good falls off the board edge or is lost when its carrier is removed (rules event "spoil")
win-fanfare.opus | win | Cheerful short brass fanfare flourish with bright jingle bells at the end | Every contract line is complete; the results screen follows (rules event "win")
lose-fall.opus | lose | Low descending trombone slide, sad comical wah falling to a quiet stop | Tick limit reached or the player resigned (rules event "lose")
undo-zip.opus | undo | Quick airy zip of a tape measure rewinding fast, light mechanical swish | Undo (Z) restores the previous state in Practice and Lesson rounds
hint-chime.opus | hint | Soft sparkling chime, gentle glass rod ping with a delicate shimmer tail | Hint (H) selects the suggested cell and explains the suggested action
star-sparkle.opus | star | Bright twinkling glockenspiel sparkle, high shimmering notes like a star appearing | Reserved for journey star awards on the results screen (not yet triggered by app.js)
mine-spawn.opus | spawn | Very soft single pebble dropping out of a wooden mine chute onto packed earth, tiny dry tock | A source emits a raw good on its cadence (rules event "spawn"); quiet so auto-run does not clutter | generated 2026-09-08
conveyor-motor.opus | auto | Small electric conveyor motor spinning up with a gentle belt slap, half a second, settling to a low purr | Auto-run toggled on or off with the button or A; the ambience hum continues underneath | generated 2026-09-08
